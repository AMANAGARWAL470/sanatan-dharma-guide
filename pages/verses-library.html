Essential Mantras & Ślokas मन्त्र एवं श्लोक पुस्तकालय

Authentic Sanskrit verses extracted from Ṛgveda, Upaniṣads, and Bhagavad Gītā with IAST transliteration, word-by-word meaning, and practical reflection.

1. Gāyatrī Mantra Ṛgveda 3.62.10
ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ॥
Oṁ bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ tat savitur vareṇyaṁ bhargo devasya dhīmahi dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt
"We meditate on the supreme effulgence of the divine Creator (Savitur). May That divine Light illuminate our intellect and inspire our thoughts."
Word-by-word: Oṁ = Primordial sound; Bhūḥ = Physical realm; Bhuvaḥ = Astral realm; Svaḥ = Celestial realm; Tat = That; Savitur = Divine Sun/Creator; Vareṇyaṁ = Most adorable/supreme; Bhargo = Radiance/illumination; Devasya = Divine; Dhīmahi = We meditate; Dhiyo = Intellect; Yo = Who; Naḥ = Our; Pracodayāt = Inspire/guide.
2. Mahāmṛtyuñjaya Mantra Ṛgveda 7.59.12 / Yajurveda 3.60
ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् । उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय माऽमृतात् ॥
Oṁ tryambakaṁ yajāmahe sugandhiṁ puṣṭi-vardhanam | urvārukamiva bandhanān mṛtyor mukṣīya mā'mṛtāt
"We worship the Three-Eyed Lord Śiva, who is fragrant and nourishes all beings. Just as a ripe cucumber is effortlessly liberated from its creeper, may He liberate us from death and bondages unto immortality."
Word-by-word: Tryambakaṁ = Three-eyed Lord; Yajāmahe = We worship; Sugandhiṁ = Fragrant; Puṣṭi-vardhanam = Nourisher of vitality; Urvārukam-iva = Like a ripe cucumber; Bandhanāt = From bondage; Mṛtyor = From death; Mukṣīya = Liberate; Mā'mṛtāt = Not from immortality.
3. Śānti Mantra (Cosmic Fullness) Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 5.1.1
ॐ पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदं पूर्णात्पूर्णमुदच्यते । पूर्णस्य पूर्णमादाय पूर्णमेवावशिष्यते ॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥
Oṁ pūrṇamadaḥ pūrṇamidaṁ pūrṇāt pūrṇamudacyate | pūrṇasya pūrṇamādāya pūrṇamevāvaśiṣyate || Oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ ||
"That Unmanifest is Full; This Manifest is Full. From Fullness emerges Fullness. When Fullness is taken from Fullness, Fullness alone remains. Om Peace, Peace, Peace."
4. Charama Śloka (The Final Refuge) Bhagavad Gītā 18.66
सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज । अहं त्वा सर्वपापेभ्यो मोक्षयिष्यामि मा शुचः ॥
sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja | ahaṁ tvā sarva-pāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ
"Abandon all egoic duties and surrender unto Me alone as your sole refuge. I shall liberate you from all sins and bondages; do not grieve."
ॐ
