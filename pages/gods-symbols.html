Why Are There Many Gods?

Simple Explanation

Sanātana Dharma teaches that God is One (Brahman), but can be experienced in many forms—just as pure white light refracts into a rainbow of seven colors through a glass prism.

💡 Everyday Analogy: Water is water. But in liquid form it is drinkable, as ice it is solid, and as steam it is invisible gas. The essence is identical; only the form changes to meet your need!

The 3 Main Cosmic Aspects (Trimūrti)

1. Brahmā

The Creator: Represents the power that brings cosmic creation into manifestation.

2. Viṣṇu

The Preserver: Maintains order, harmony, and righteousness in the universe.

3. Śiva

The Regenerator: Dissolves ego, illusion, and darkness so new life can emerge.

Sacred Symbols & What They Mean

AUM (ॐ)

The primordial sound vibration of creation. Encompasses all speech, creation, and silence.

Swastika (卐)

Ancient Vedic symbol of good fortune, health, and cosmic balance (from Su-asti = well-being).

Lotus (Padma)

Symbol of purity. Grows out of muddy water without a single drop of mud sticking to it.
