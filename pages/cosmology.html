Kāla Cakra · Astronomical Cosmic Scales

Vedic Cosmology & Time Cycles काल चक्र एवं ब्रह्माण्ड

Extracted from Viṣṇu Purāṇa, Śrīmad Bhāgavata Purāṇa (3rd & 5th Skandhas), and Sūrya Siddhānta. Vedic cosmology measures time on immense, cyclical scales mirroring modern astrophysics.

Mathematical Units of Time

The 4 Yugas (Cosmic Ages) चतुर्युग

1 Human Year = 1 Day of the Devas. The 4 Yugas follow a 4:3:2:1 ratio:

4,800 Divine Years

Satya Yuga

1,728,000 Human Years. Full virtue. Lifespan long, mind naturally established in meditation.

3,600 Divine Years

Tretā Yuga

1,296,000 Human Years. Virtue drops to 3/4. Era of Sri Rama and sacrificial rites (*Yajna*).

2,400 Divine Years

Dvāpara Yuga

864,000 Human Years. Virtue drops to 1/2. Era of Sri Krishna & temple worship.

1,200 Divine Years

Kali Yuga

432,000 Human Years. Current era (began 3102 BCE at Krishna's departure). Virtue at 1/4 (Truth).

Immense Cosmic Eras

Kalpas & Manvantaras कल्प एवं मन्वन्तर

  • 1 Mahāyuga: Satya + Tretā + Dvāpara + Kali = 4,320,000 Human Years.
  • 1 Kalpa (1 Day of Brahmā): 1,000 Mahāyugas = 4,320,000,000 (4.32 Billion) Years. During Brahmā's day, physical universe manifests.
  • 1 Pralaya (1 Night of Brahmā): Equal to 4.32 Billion Years of unmanifest cosmic sleep.
  • Manvantara: Each Kalpa is divided into 14 Manvantaras (ruled by 14 consecutive Manus). 1 Manvantara = 71 Mahāyugas (~306.72 Million Years).
  • Current Cosmic Address: We are currently in the Śvetavarāha Kalpa, during the 7th Manvantara (ruled by Vaivasvata Manu), in the 28th Mahāyuga, during the early phase of Kali Yuga.
Cosmic Dimensions

The 14 Lokas (Realms of Being) चतुर्दश लोक

Vedic cosmology maps 14 plane dimensions existing simultaneously:

7 Higher Spheres (Urdhva Loka)
  • Satyaloka (Brahmaloka): Realm of pure truth & Brahmā.
  • Tapoloka: Realm of supreme ascetics & immortal seers.
  • Janaloka: Realm of enlightened souls.
  • Maharloka: Realm of great seers (Sages Bhrigu, Marichi).
  • Svarloka (Svarga): Heavenly realm of Devas (Indra).
  • Bhuvarloka: Atmospheric intermediate plane.
  • Bhūloka: Physical Earth plane.
7 Lower Spheres (Adho Loka)
  • Atala: Realm of sensual enchantments.
  • Vitala: Realm of gold & subterranean powers.
  • Sutala: Realm ruled by virtuous King Bali.
  • Talātala: Realm of mystical architecture (Maya Danava).
  • Mahātala: Realm of great serpents (Nagas).
  • Rasātala: Realm of Daityas & Danavas.
  • Pātāla: Subterranean realm ruled by Vasuki.
