Apauruṣeya · Eternal Sound Vibrations

The Four Vedas & Vedic Science चतुर्वेद

The Vedas (from root Vid = to know) are classified as Apauruṣeya—not created by human authorship, but perceived as eternal sound vibrations (*Śabda Brahman*) by ancient Rishis during deep meditation (*Tapas*).

Primary Veda · 1,028 Suktas · 10 Mandalas

1. Ṛgveda Saṃhitā ऋग्वेद संहिता

The oldest extant text in human history (1,636 pages in complete Sakala Shakha). It contains 10,552 mantras celebrating cosmic order (*Ṛta*), fire (*Agni*), light (*Sūrya*), dawn (*Uṣas*), and ultimate unity.

Famous Hymns of the Ṛgveda

Nāsadīya Sūkta (Hymn of Creation) Ṛgveda 10.129
नासदासीन्नो सदासीत्तदानीं नासीद्रजो नो व्योमा परो यत् । किमावरीवः कुह कस्य शर्मन्नम्भः किमासीद्गहनं गभीरम् ॥
"Then was neither Non-existence nor Existence; there was no realm of air, nor the sky beyond. What covered it, and where? What was the shelter? Was there water, unfathomably deep?"
Cosmic Insight: Anticipates modern quantum physics and cosmology—describing a state prior to space, time, matter, and dualities where the One breathed breathless by Its own power.
Puruṣa Sūkta (The Cosmic Being) Ṛgveda 10.90
सहस्रशीर्षा पुरुषः सहस्राक्षः सहस्रपात् । स भूमिं विश्वतो वृत्वात्यतिष्ठद्दशांगुलम् ॥
"The Cosmic Being has a thousand heads, a thousand eyes, and a thousand feet. Enveloping the universe on every side, He transcends it by ten fingers' breadth."
Practical Veda · 20 Kandas · 730 Hymns

2. Atharvaveda Saṃhitā अथर्ववेद संहिता

Preserved by sages Atharvan and Angiras (616 pages in Jayadev Sharma edition). Contains practical formulas for health, governance, harmony, ecology, and home protection. Forms the foundational root of Āyurveda.

Bhūmi Sūkta (Hymn to Mother Earth) Atharvaveda 12.1.12
माता भूमिः पुत्रो अहं पृथिव्याः ।
"The Earth is my Mother; I am the child of the Earth."
Vedic Ecology: Ancient proclamation of environmental stewardship and sacred relationship with nature.
The Musical Veda

3. Sāmaveda Saṃhitā सामवेद

The Veda of sacred melodies and chants. Lord Kṛṣṇa proclaims in Gītā 10.22: "Among the Vedas I am the Sāmaveda." Forms the historical foundation of Indian Classical Music (*Rāgas*).

The Ritual Veda

4. Yajurveda Saṃhitā यजुर्वेद

Contains prose mantras for fire sacrifices (*Yajna*). Divided into Śukla Yajurveda (White) and Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda (Black). Houses the Śatarudrīya and Īśā Upaniṣad.

The 4 Layers of Every Veda

Layer 1

Saṃhitā

The core collection of poetic hymns, prayers, and invocations to divine cosmic powers.

Layer 2

Brāhmaṇa

Prose manuals explaining the procedures, symbolism, and science behind fire sacrifices (*Yajna*).

Layer 3

Āraṇyaka

"Forest treatises" focusing on internal mental worship and symbolic meaning of rituals.

Layer 4

Upaniṣad

Philosophical climax (Vedānta) dealing with ultimate knowledge of Brahman, Ātman, and Mokṣa.
