10 Common Misconceptions Busted

Click any myth below to reveal the authentic scriptural truth in simple English:

❌ Myth 1: "Hindus worship 330 million different Gods."
+
✓ Scriptural Fact

The Vedas explicitly state God is One (*Ekam Sat*). The Sanskrit phrase "33 Koti Deva" was mistranslated in the 19th century—Koti here means "types" or "categories" of divine cosmic manifestations (like sun, wind, water, light), NOT "crores" (millions)!

❌ Myth 2: "The Caste System is mandated by birth in original scriptures."
+
✓ Scriptural Fact

False! Bhagavad Gītā 4.13 clearly states that Varṇa (aptitude) is based strictly on Guṇa (character/nature) and Karma (action), NEVER by birth. Discriminatory birth-caste is a socio-political corruption condemned by ancient seers.

❌ Myth 3: "Sanatanis worship idols/stones as God."
+
✓ Scriptural Fact

Sanatanis do not worship the stone; they use consecrated sacred forms (*Mūrti*) as a focus to worship the Omnipresent God IN the form. Just as you look at a photograph of a family member to feel love for them, a Mūrti helps anchor focus during prayer.

❌ Myth 4: "Sanatan Dharma demands everyone become an ascetic monk."
+
✓ Scriptural Fact

Not at all! Sanātana Dharma outlines 4 Puruṣārthas (Life Goals): Ethics (*Dharma*), Wealth/Success (*Artha*), Joy/Pleasure (*Kāma*), and Freedom (*Mokṣa*). It encourages enjoying family life, careers, and wealth honestly!

❌ Myth 5: "Women had no spiritual or intellectual status in Vedic times."
+
✓ Scriptural Fact

Vedic literature features revered female seers (*Rishikās*) like Gargi, Maitreyi, Lopamudra, and Aditi who debated great sages, composed Vedic mantras, and led educational assemblies.
