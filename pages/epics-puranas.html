The Great Epics & 18 Purāṇas इतिहास एवं पुराण

While the Vedas contain abstract philosophical truths, the Itihāsas (Epics) and Purāṇas transmit these eternal truths to every household through narrative, history, genealogy, and sacred symbolism.

Author: Sage Vedavyāsa · 100,000 Verses · 18 Parvas

1. The Mahābhārata महाभारत

Extracted from the complete 10-Volume text (54.4 MB). Known as the Pañcama Veda (5th Veda). Recounts the epic conflict between the Pāṇḍavas and Kauravas, containing deep treatises on statecraft (*Rājadharma*), law, ethics, and liberation (*Mokṣadharma*).

The 18 Parvas (Books) of Mahābhārata

Book 01

Ādi Parva

Origins of Kuru dynasty, birth of heroes, & snake sacrifice.

Book 02

Sabhā Parva

Royal assembly hall, dice game, & exile of Pandavas.

Book 03

Vana Parva

12 years forest exile & stories of Nala-Damayanti, Savitri.

Book 04

Virāṭa Parva

13th year incognito disguise in King Virata's court.

Book 05

Udyoga Parva

Peace negotiations & Sanjaya/Krishna peace missions.

Book 06

Bhīṣma Parva

First 10 days of war; houses the complete Bhagavad Gītā.

1,590 Pages · 12 Skandhas · 18,000 Verses

2. Śrīmad Bhāgavata Purāṇa श्रीमद्भागवत पुराण

Extracted from Gita Press edition. Considered the crown jewel of Puranic literature. Recounted by Sage Śukadeva to King Parīkṣit. Details the 24 Avatars of Vishnu, cosmic cycles, and the divine play (*Līlā*) of Sri Krishna.

धर्मः प्रोज्झितकैतवोऽत्र परमो निर्मत्सराणां सतां वेद्यं वास्तवमत्र वस्तु शिवदं तापत्रयोन्मूलनम् ॥
"In this Bhagavata Purana, all deceitful practices of religion are completely rejected. Here is revealed the Supreme Reality for pure-hearted seekers, destroying the 3 forms of suffering."
1,240 Pages · Gita Press

3. Śiva Purāṇa शिव पुराण

Divided into Samhitas (Vidyeshvara, Rudra, Sati, Parvati, Shatarudra, Kotirudra, Uma, Kailasa). Details Linga origin, worship rites, and 12 Jyotirlingas.

424 Pages · H.H. Wilson

4. Viṣṇu Purāṇa विष्णु पुराण

Classical 6-part Purana explaining creation (*Sarga*), dissolution (*Pratisarga*), royal dynasties (*Vaṁśa*), Yuga cycles, and Dhruva/Prahlada legends.

1,762 Pages · 2 Volumes · Gita Press

5. Devī Bhāgavata Purāṇa देवीभागवत पुराण

Primary scripture of Śāktism detailing the supreme status of the Divine Mother (Śakti), her manifestation as Mahālakṣmī, Mahākālī, and Mahāsarasvatī, the 51 Śaktipīṭhas, and the Navarātrī vow.
