Dharmaśāstra Canon · Purification Sacraments

The 16 Saṃskāras & Daily Ethics षोडश संस्कार

Extracted from classical Gṛhyasūtras and Dharmaśāstras (8232 & 8254 editions). Saṃskāra means purificatory rite that refines human consciousness at key milestones of life.

Category 1: Prenatal Rites (Prāk-Janma Saṃskāras)

Sacrament 01

1. Garbhādhāna गर्भाधान

Sacred prayerful invocation before conception to invite a pure, noble soul into the mother's womb.

Sacrament 02

2. Puṁsavana पुंसवन

Performed during the 3rd month of pregnancy for the physical and mental health of the growing fetus.

Sacrament 03

3. Sīmantonnayana सीमन्तोन्नयन

Parting of the mother's hair in the 7th month, accompanied by uplifting music and prayers for mental tranquility.

Category 2: Childhood Rites (Bālya Saṃskāras)

Sacrament 04

Jātakarma

Birth ceremony. Whispering AUM and truth into the newborn's ear with honey.

Sacrament 05

Nāmakaraṇa

Formal naming ceremony performed on the 11th or 12th day after birth.

Sacrament 06

Niṣkramaṇa

Child's first outing into nature to behold the sun and moon in the 4th month.

Sacrament 07

Annaprāśana

First feeding of solid sanctified food (kheer/rice) in the 6th month.

Sacrament 08

Cūḍākaraṇa

First tonsure (shaving of head) to promote brain development and purity.

Sacrament 09

Karṇavedha

Ear-piercing rite for health benefits and aesthetic adornment.

Category 3: Educational & Adult Rites (Śikṣā & Gṛhastha Saṃskāras)

Sacrament 10

10. Vidyārambha विद्यारम्भ

Child's initiation into learning the alphabet and writing first letters in rice grains.

Sacrament 11

11. Upanayana (Sacred Thread) उपनयन

Initiation into formal Vedic studies, self-discipline, and receiving the Gāyatrī Mantra from the Guru. Symbolizes spiritual second birth (*Dvija*).

Sacrament 12

12. Vedārambha वेदारम्भ

Commencement of formal recitation and deep study of the Veda Saṃhitās.

Sacrament 13

13. Keśānta (Godāna) केशान्त

First shaving of beard at age 16, marking transition to adulthood.

Sacrament 14

14. Samāvartana समावर्तन

Graduation ceremony upon completing education and returning home from the Gurukula.

Sacrament 15

15. Vivāha (Sacred Marriage) विवाह

Sacred union of husband and wife before the holy fire (*Saptapadī* — 7 steps), entering the Householder stage to walk the path of Dharma together.

Sacrament 16

16. Antyeṣṭi (Last Rites) अन्त्येष्टि

Cremation and funeral rites returning the physical body's 5 elements back to nature while praying for the soul's ultimate liberation.
