The 5 Big Ideas Made Super Simple

If you understand these 5 terms, you understand 90% of Sanātana Dharma philosophy!

1. Brahman (The Infinite Source)

What it is: The one ultimate, infinite reality behind the whole universe. It is not an old man in the clouds, but the pure energy and consciousness that enlivens everything.
💡 Everyday Analogy: Think of Brahman as the vast ocean. Every living being, star, and planet is like a wave arising from that same ocean.

2. Ātman (Your True Inner Self)

What it is: Your true, immortal soul inside. You are not just your body or your thoughts; you are the conscious witness observing them.
💡 Everyday Analogy: If Brahman is the big ocean, your Ātman is a single drop of water from that ocean. In essence, the drop IS the ocean!

3. Karma (The Law of Cause & Effect)

What it is: Every action, word, and thought has a consequence. Good actions create positive energy; harmful actions create negative energy.
💡 Everyday Analogy: Karma is like a boomerang or an echo. Whatever you throw into the world eventually circles back to you.

4. Māyā (The Cosmic Illusion)

What it is: The illusion that makes us feel completely separate from God and each other.
💡 Everyday Analogy: Imagine putting on a VR (Virtual Reality) Headset. The game feels so real that you forget you are sitting safely in your bedroom. Māyā is that cosmic VR game!

5. Mokṣa (Ultimate Freedom)

What it is: Taking off the VR headset! Realizing your true divine nature and breaking free from all suffering, fear, and reincarnation.
💡 Everyday Analogy: Like a wave realizing it was never separate from the peaceful ocean all along.
