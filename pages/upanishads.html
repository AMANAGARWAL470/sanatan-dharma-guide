Śruti Prasthāna · Philosophical Peak

The 10 Principal Upaniṣads मुख्य उपनिषद्

The Upaniṣads (sitting near the teacher to receive secret wisdom) represent the culmination of the Vedas (Vedānta). Out of 108 traditional Upaniṣads, Ādi Śaṅkarācārya authored commentaries on the 10 Mukhya (Principal) Upaniṣads.

Śukla Yajurveda · Largest Upaniṣad

1. Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad बृहदारण्यक उपनिषद्

Famous for the dialogues of Sage Yajñavalkya with his scholarly wife Maitreyī and King Janaka. Contains the famous Pavamāna Mantra and the Mahāvākya "Ahaṃ Brahmāsmi".

असतो मा सद्गमय । तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय । मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय ॥
asato mā sad-gamaya | tamaso mā jyotir-gamaya | mṛtyor-mā amṛtaṁ gamaya
"Lead me from the unreal to the Real; Lead me from darkness to Light; Lead me from death to Immortality."
Sāmaveda · Melodic Wisdom

2. Chāndogya Upaniṣad छान्दोग्य उपनिषद्

Contains the profound dialogue between Sage Uddālaka and his son Śvetaketu where the Mahāvākya "Tat Tvam Asi" (That Thou Art) is repeated 9 times with analogies of honey, rivers, and seeds.

स य एषोऽणिमैतदात्म्यमिदं सर्वं तत्सत्यं स आत्मा तत्त्वमसि श्वेतकेतो ॥
sa ya eṣo'ṇimaitadātmyamidaṁ sarvaṁ tatsatyaṁ sa ātmā tattvamasi śvetaketo
"That subtle essence which is the truth of all existence, that is the Self, and That Thou Art, O Śvetaketu!"
Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda · Dialogue with Death

3. Kaṭha Upaniṣad कठ उपनिषद्

Narrates the story of young Nachiketa who travels to the abode of Yama (Lord of Death) and asks for the ultimate boon: What happens to a soul after death? Yama reveals the nature of Ātman and the Chariot Analogy.

आत्मानं रथिनं विद्धि शरीरं रथमेव तु । बुद्धिं तु सारथिं विद्धि मनः प्रग्रहमेव च ॥
ātmānaṁ rathinaṁ viddhi śarīraṁ rathameva tu | buddhiṁ tu sārathiṁ viddhi manaḥ pragrahameva ca
"Know the Self as the rider in the chariot, and the body as the chariot. Know the intellect (Buddhi) as the charioteer, and the mind as the reins."
Śukla Yajurveda · All-Pervasive Reality

4. Īśāvāsya Upaniṣad ईशावास्य उपनिषद्

The 40th chapter of the Śukla Yajurveda. Teaches how to live in the world with detachment by seeing everything enfolded in the Divine.

ईशा वास्यमिदं सर्वं यत्किञ्च जगत्यां जगत् । तेन त्यक्तेन भुञ्जीथा मा गृधः कस्यस्विद्धनम् ॥
īśā vāsyamidaṁ sarvaṁ yatkiñca jagatyāṁ jagat | tena tyaktena bhuñjīthā mā gṛdhaḥ kasyasviddhanam
"All this—whatever exists in this changing universe—is enveloped by the Lord. Enjoy through detachment; do not covet anyone's wealth."
Atharvaveda · Analysis of Consciousness & AUM

5. Māṇḍūkya Upaniṣad माण्डूक्य उपनिषद्

Consisting of only 12 verses, it is considered the most concentrated exposition of Vedānta. Analyzes the 4 States of Consciousness:

  • Jāgrat (Waking): Experience of external material world (represented by letter 'A').
  • Svapna (Dreaming): Internal mental projections (represented by letter 'U').
  • Suṣupti (Deep Sleep): Unified bliss state without mental images (represented by letter 'M').
  • Turīya (The Fourth): Pure non-dual Consciousness underlying all states (represented by silence after AUM).

Remaining Mukhya Upaniṣads

Sāmaveda

Kena Upaniṣad

Inquires: "By whom impelled does the mind fly to its objects?" Reveals that Brahman is the power behind senses.

Atharvaveda

Muṇḍaka Upaniṣad

Contains the Two Birds Analogy (Jiva & Ishvara) and the famous national motto: "Satyameva Jayate" (Truth Alone Triumphs).

Atharvaveda

Praśna Upaniṣad

Six questions asked by six seers to Sage Pippalāda concerning the origin of life, Prāna, and supreme realization.

Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda

Taittirīya Upaniṣad

Explores the 5 Sheaths of existence (Pañcakośa: Annamaya, Pranamaya, Manomaya, Vijnanamaya, Anandamaya).

Ṛgveda

Aitareya Upaniṣad

Presents cosmic creation and the Mahāvākya "Prajñānaṁ Brahma" (Consciousness is Brahman).

Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda

Śvetāśvatara Upaniṣad

Synthesizes Vedānta, Sāṃkhya, and Yoga with intense personal devotion (Bhakti) to Lord Śiva (Rudra).
