Sacred Books Explained Simply

There are thousands of ancient texts. But don't worry! They fall into 2 easy categories:

Category 1 · Direct Discovery

Śruti (Direct Revealed Knowledge)

Eternal truths discovered by seers in deep meditation. These never change.

📖 Includes: The 4 Vedas & The Upaniṣads.

Category 2 · Stories & Ethics

Smṛti (Remembered & Applied Texts)

Stories, history, epics, and practical codes that adapt to different times and ages.

📖 Includes: Rāmāyaṇa, Mahābhārata, Bhagavad Gītā, & Purāṇas.

⭐ Which Book Should You Read First as a Beginner?

Start with the Bhagavad Gītā!

The Bhagavad Gītā is a 700-verse conversation between Sri Krishna and Arjuna on the battlefield. It summarizes all the wisdom of the Vedas into practical, everyday advice on handling stress, performing duty, mastering the mind, and finding inner peace.
