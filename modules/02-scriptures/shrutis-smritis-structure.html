Lesson 2.4 of Module 02 · Master Reference Taxonomy

Śruti vs Smṛti — The Complete Map of Sacred Literature

📖 Primary Source: Manusmriti 2.10 · Rig Veda Sakala Shakha · 108 Upanishads  | 📚 Read in Library →
श्रुतिस्तु वेदः विज्ञेयो धर्मशास्त्रं तु वै स्मृतः ।
ते सर्वार्थेष्वमीमांस्ये ताभ्यां धर्मो हि निर्बभौ ॥
"By Śruti is meant the Veda, and by Smṛti the books of Law. These two are beyond dispute in all matters, for from them springs Dharma."
— Manusmriti 2.10 (8232.pdf, p. 18)

The 2 Primary Categories

Category 1 · Unchangeable Authority

1. Śruti (श्रुति) — "That Which Was Heard"

The eternal, uncreated cosmic truths revealed to ancient Rishis in deep meditation. Includes the 4 Vedas (Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva) and their 4 internal layers: Saṃhitās, Brāhmaṇas, Āraṇyakas, and Upaniṣads. Śruti is primary authority.

Category 2 · Contextual Guidance

2. Smṛti (स्मृति) — "That Which Is Remembered"

Humanly remembered and recorded texts that apply Vedic wisdom to changing social ages. Includes the 6 Vedāṅgas, 4 Upavedas, 18 Purāṇas, Rāmāyaṇa & Mahābhārata (Itihāsa), and 6 Darśanas. If Smṛti conflicts with Śruti, Śruti prevails.

The 4 Layers of Each Veda

Layer 1

Saṃhitā

Hymns, mantras, and sound vibrations chanted in praise of divine cosmic forces.

Layer 2

Brāhmaṇa

Prose explanations of rituals, fire ceremonies (Yajña), and symbolic meaning.

Layer 3

Āraṇyaka

"Forest texts" written for meditative retreat, inward reflection, and symbolic rites.

Layer 4

Upaniṣad

Vedānta ("end of Veda") — high philosophical dialogues on Brahman, Ātman, and Mokṣa.
