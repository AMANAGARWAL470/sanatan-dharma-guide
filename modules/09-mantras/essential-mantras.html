Essential Mantras Every Sanātani Should Know

📖 Primary Sources: Rig Veda Complete (Sakala Shakha) · Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad  | 📚 Read in Library →

A Mantra (मन्त्र) comes from Sanskrit: Man (मनस् = mind) + Tra (त्रा = protecting, freeing) — that which frees the mind from limitation. Below are three of the most sacred mantras in Sanātana Dharma with their exact Sanskrit, IAST transliteration, and word-by-word meanings.

1. The Gāyatrī Mantra — Prayer for Intellect

ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः ।
तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि ।
धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ॥
om bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ
tat savitur vareṇyaṁ bhargo devasya dhīmahi
dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt
"We meditate on the supreme splendor of the Divine Sun (Savitur) — may That Divine Being illuminate and inspire our intellect."
— Rig Veda, Maṇḍala 3, Sūkta 62, Mantra 10  |  Source: Rig Veda Complete (Sakala Shakha), pg. 380

2. Mahāmṛtyuñjaya Mantra — Conquest of Fear & Death

ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम् ।
उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय माऽमृतात् ॥
om tryambakaṁ yajāmahe sugandhiṁ puṣṭi-vardhanam
urvārukam iva bandhanān mṛtyor mukṣīya mā 'mṛtāt
"We worship the Three-Eyed Lord (Śiva), who is fragrant and nourishes all beings. Just as a ripe cucumber is liberated from its vine, may He liberate us from death into immortality."
— Rig Veda, Maṇḍala 7, Sūkta 59, Mantra 12  |  Source: Rig Veda Complete (Sakala Shakha), pg. 840

3. Pavamāna Abhyāroha — From Darkness to Light

ॐ असतो मा सद्गमय ।
तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय ।
मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय ॥
ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥
om asato mā sad gamaya
tamaso mā jyotir gamaya
mṛtyor mā amṛtaṁ gamaya
om śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ
"Lead me from falsehood to truth. Lead me from darkness to light. Lead me from death to immortality. Om Peace, Peace, Peace."
— Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad 1.3.28  |  Source: Brihadaranyaka-Upanishad.pdf (987 pp), pg. 120
