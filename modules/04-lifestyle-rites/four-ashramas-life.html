Lesson 4.3 of Module 04 · Stages of Life

The 4 Stages of Human Life — Catur-Āśrama

📖 Primary Sources: Manusmriti Chapters 2–6 · Jabala Upanishad · Bhagavad Gītā  | 📚 Read in Library →

Sanātana Dharma structures human life into 4 distinct 25-year phases (*Āśramas*) to ensure that psychological growth, career development, social responsibility, and spiritual contemplation unfold naturally.

Stage 1 · Years 0 to 25

1. Brahmacarya (Student Phase)

Focus on education, character building, physical discipline, study of scriptures, and self-control. The student lives with humility and absorbs knowledge.

Stage 2 · Years 25 to 50

2. Gṛhastha (Householder Phase)

The backbone of society. Marriage, raising children, earning an honest career (*Artha*), enjoying ethical pleasure (*Kāma*), and supporting the other 3 stages.

Stage 3 · Years 50 to 75

3. Vānaprastha (Retirement & Mentorship)

Gradual transition from active material ambitions to mentorship, community service, and deeper scriptural contemplation as children become independent.

Stage 4 · Years 75+

4. Saṃnyāsa (Renunciation & Freedom)

Complete spiritual devotion. Renouncing personal attachments, ego, and worldly vanity to focus 100% on Mokṣa and realization of the eternal Ātman.
