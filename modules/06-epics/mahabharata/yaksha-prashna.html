Dense Philosophical Dialogue · Vana Parva 312–313

Yakṣa Praśna — The 60 Moral Riddles of Dharma

📖 Primary Source: Mahābhārata, Vana Parva, Chapters 312–313 (The Mahābhārata Set of 10 Volumes)  | 📚 Read in Library →

During their 12th year of forest exile, the Pāṇḍavas chase a deer and grow thirsty. One by one, Nakula, Sahadeva, Arjuna, and Bhīma approach a pristine crane-guarded lake. A mysterious voice (*Yakṣa*) warns: "Answer my questions before drinking!" They ignore the voice, drink, and collapse dead. Finally, Yudhiṣṭhira arrives, sees his brothers dead, and humbly agrees to answer all questions. The Yakṣa is **Dharma incarnate** testing Yudhiṣṭhira's wisdom.

Riddle 1: What is heavier than Earth? What is higher than Heaven?
किं स्विद् गुरुतरं भूमेः? किं स्विद् उच्चतरं च खात्?
"Yudhiṣṭhira answered: The **Mother** is heavier than Earth. The **Father** is higher than Heaven."
— Mahābhārata, Vana Parva 313.60
Riddle 2: What moves faster than Wind? What is more numerous than Straw?
किं स्विद् शीघ्रतरं वातात? किं स्विद् बहुतरं तृणात्?
"Yudhiṣṭhira answered: The **Mind** moves faster than Wind. **Thoughts / Worries** are more numerous than straw."
— Mahābhārata, Vana Parva 313.61
Riddle 3: What is the greatest wonder in the world?
अहन्यहनि भूतानि गच्छन्तीह यमालयम् ।
शेषाः स्थावरमिच्छन्ति किमाश्चर्यमतः परम् ॥
"Yudhiṣṭhira answered: Every single day, countless beings pass away to the house of Death. Yet those who remain act as if they will live forever. What can be a greater wonder than this?"
— Mahābhārata, Vana Parva 313.116
Riddle 4: What is the true path to wisdom?
तर्कोऽप्रतिष्ठः श्रुतयो विभिन्ना नैको ऋषिर्यस्य मतं प्रमाणम् ।
धर्मस्य तत्त्वं निहितं गुहायां महाजनो येन गतः स पन्थाः ॥
"Yudhiṣṭhira answered: Logic is inconclusive; scriptures differ; no single Rishi's opinion is final. The secret of Dharma is hidden in the heart. The true path is the one walked by noble souls (*Mahājanas*)."
— Mahābhārata, Vana Parva 313.117
