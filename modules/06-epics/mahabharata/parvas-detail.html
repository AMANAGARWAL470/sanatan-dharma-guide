Exhaustive Breakdown of All 18 Parvas & The 18-Day War

📖 Primary Source: The Mahābhārata Set of 10 Volumes (671,014 lines, 100,000 verses)  | 📚 Read in Library →

Day-by-Day Timeline of the 18-Day Kurukṣetra War

Days 1 to 10 · Commander: Bhīṣma

Bhīṣma's Commandership

• Day 1: Demoralization of Pāṇḍavas; Śalya kills Uttara.
• Day 3: Kṛṣṇa rushes at Bhīṣma with chariot wheel to spur Arjuna.
• Day 9: Bhīṣma tells Yudhiṣṭhira how he can be defeated (by placing Śikhaṇḍī in front).
• Day 10: Arjuna shoots arrows behind Śikhaṇḍī. Bhīṣma falls on a bed of arrows (*Śarasayyā*).

Days 11 to 15 · Commander: Droṇācārya

Droṇa's Commandership

• Day 11–12: Droṇa tries to capture Yudhiṣṭhira alive; Bhagadatta's Vaishnavastra intercepted by Kṛṣṇa.
• Day 13: Formation of Chakravyūha; heroic last stand and death of 16-year-old Abhimanyu.
• Day 14: Arjuna's vow to slay Jayadratha before sunset; Kṛṣṇa hides sun temporarily.
• Day 15: Slaying of Droṇa after news of *"Aśvatthāmā is dead"* (the elephant).

Days 16 to 17 · Commander: Karṇa

Karṇa's Commandership

• Day 16: Karṇa takes command; Śalya acts as his reluctant chariot driver.
• Day 17: Bhīma slays Duḥśāsana and fulfills Draupadī's vow. Epic final duel between Arjuna and Karṇa. Karṇa's chariot wheel sinks into mud due to a curse; Arjuna slays Karṇa.

Day 18 & Night · Commanders: Śalya & Aśvatthāmā

Final Victory & Night Massacre

• Day 18 (Morning): Yudhiṣṭhira slays Śalya. Sahadeva slays Śakuni.
• Day 18 (Afternoon): Duryodhana hides in Dvaipāyana lake; Bhīma defeats him in mace duel (*Gadā-yuddha*).
• Night 18: Aśvatthāmā conducts illegal night raid, slaying the 5 Upapāṇḍavas (Draupadī's sons).
