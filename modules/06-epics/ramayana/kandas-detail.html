Exhaustive Breakdown of the 7 Kāṇḍas

📖 Primary Source: Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa (user index.pdf — 1,626 pages, 24,000 verses, 500 Sargas)  | 📚 Read in Library →
Kāṇḍa 1 of 7 · 77 Sargas · ~2,000 Verses

1. Bāla Kāṇḍa (Book of Childhood & Divine Incarnation)

Geographical Path: Ayodhyā (Uttar Pradesh) → Siddhāśrama (Buxar, Bihar) → Janakpur (Nepal).

Key Sargas & Milestones:

• Sargas 1–4: Maharṣi Vālmīki's encounter with Nārada; composition of the Ādikāvya in Anuṣṭubh metre after seeing a Krauñca bird slain by a hunter.
• Sargas 8–18:Putrakāmeṣṭi Yajna by King Daśaratha and birth of Rāma, Bharata, Lakṣmaṇa, and Śatrughna.
• Sargas 19–30: Arrival of Sage Viśvāmitra. Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa accompany him to protect sacred rituals. Slaying of Tāṭakā and Subāhu.
• Sargas 48–49: Redemption of Ahalyā at Gautama's Āśrama.
• Sargas 66–77: Breaking of Śiva's Bow (*Pināka*) in Mithilā; marriage of Rāma-Sītā, Lakṣmaṇa-Urmilā, Bharata-Māṇḍavī, and Śatrughna-Śrutakīrti. Encounter with Paraśurāma on the return journey.

Kāṇḍa 2 of 7 · 119 Sargas · ~4,300 Verses

2. Ayodhyā Kāṇḍa (Book of Ayodhya & Unflinching Duty)

Geographical Path: Ayodhyā → Śṛṅgaverapura (Ganga river crossing) → Prayāgarāja → Chitrakooṭa (Madhya Pradesh).

Key Sargas & Milestones:

• Sargas 1–9: Decision for coronation; Mantharā's manipulation of Kaikeyī.
• Sargas 10–19: Kaikeyī demands the two boons (Exile for Rāma, Throne for Bharata). Śrī Rāma accepts without a tremor of resentment: "Father's word is supreme."
• Sargas 26–31: Sītā and Lakṣmaṇa demand to accompany Rāma into the forest.
• Sargas 45–52: Departure from Ayodhyā; Guha the Niṣāda king meets Rāma at Śṛṅgaverapura.
• Sargas 64–66: Death of King Daśaratha due to grief (*Putra-śoka*).
• Sargas 99–112: The Chitrakooṭa Assembly (*Rāma-Bharata Saṃvāda*): Bharata tries to bring Rāma back. Rāma refuses to break their father's vow. Bharata takes Rāma's wooden sandals (*Pādukā*) and rules from Nandigrāma for 14 years as an ascetic regent.

Kāṇḍa 3 of 7 · 75 Sargas · ~2,400 Verses

3. Araṇya Kāṇḍa (Book of the Forest & Sītā's Abduction)

Geographical Path: Chitrakooṭa → Daṇḍakāraṇya → Pañcavaṭī (Nashik, Maharashtra).

Key Sargas & Milestones:

• Sargas 1–11: Deep forest exile. Vows to Rishis to eliminate demon terror in Daṇḍakāraṇya.
• Sargas 17–30: Śūrpaṇakhā's disfigurement by Lakṣmaṇa; slaying of 14,000 demons led by Khara and Dūṣaṇa.
• Sargas 35–44: Rāvaṇa enlists Mārīca (golden deer). Mārīca lures Rāma away.
• Sargas 45–56: Abduction of Sītā: Rāvaṇa disguised as a mendicant abducts Sītā. **Jaṭāyu's battle**: the elderly vulture king cuts Rāvaṇa's weapons before having his wings severed.
• Sargas 67–74: Rāma performs Jaṭāyu's last rites; meets Śabarī at Lake Pampā. Śabarī offers tasted berries (*Bhakti*).

Kāṇḍa 4 of 7 · 67 Sargas · ~2,400 Verses

4. Kiṣkindhā Kāṇḍa (Book of the Monkey Kingdom & Alliances)

Geographical Path: Lake Pampā → Ṛṣyamūka Hill (Hampi, Karnataka) → Kishkindha.

Key Sargas & Milestones:

• Sargas 1–4: First meeting of Śrī Rāma and Śrī Hanumān. Hanumān's refined speech impresses Rāma.
• Sargas 5–12: Alliance with Sugrīva: Sacred fire as witness for mutual pledge of alliance.
• Sargas 16–26: Slaying of Vālī: Rāma slays Vālī from behind a tree. Vālī's dying dialogue on Dharma and Rāma's justification.
• Sargas 40–49: 4-Directional Search: Sugrīva mobilizes Vānara legions. Hanumān, Aṅgada, and Jāmbavān sent South.
• Sargas 56–67: Sampāti (Jaṭāyu's brother) confirms Sītā is in Laṅkā. Jāmbavān awakens Hanumān's forgotten strength.

Kāṇḍa 5 of 7 · 68 Sargas · ~2,800 Verses

5. Sundara Kāṇḍa (Book of Heroism, Beauty & Hanumān's Feats)

Geographical Path: Mahendra Giri → 100 Yojanas Ocean Leap → Laṅkā (Sri Lanka).

Key Sargas & Milestones:

• Sargas 1–3: The Ocean Leap: Overcoming Maināka, Surasā, and Siṃhikā. Defeating Laṅkiṇī at Laṅkā's gate.
• Sargas 13–19: Discovery of Sītā: Hanumān searches palace, finds Sītā in Aśoka Vaṭikā under Rakṣasīs guard.
• Sargas 33–38: Giving the Ring (*Aṅgulīyaka*): Hanumān chants Rāma's story from a tree, presents signet ring. Sītā gives *Cūḍāmaṇi* (crest jewel) in return.
• Sargas 41–55: Destruction of Laṅkā: Hanumān destroys Aśoka Vaṭikā, slays Akṣaya Kumāra, captured by Indrajīt's Brahmāstra, speaks Dharma in Rāvaṇa's court, sets fire to Laṅkā with burning tail.
• Sargas 56–68: Return & "Dṛṣṭā Sītā": Returns to Mahendra Giri. Hanumān utters the famous words to Rāma: "Dṛṣṭā Sītā" ("Seen has been Sītā").

Kāṇḍa 6 of 7 · 128 Sargas · ~4,400 Verses

6. Yuddha Kāṇḍa (Book of War & The Triumph of Dharma)

Geographical Path: Rameshwaram → Rāma Setu → Battleground of Laṅkā → Ayodhyā.

Key Sargas & Milestones:

• Sargas 17–19: Vibhīṣaṇa's Refuge (*Śaraṇāgati*): Rāvaṇa rejects Vibhīṣaṇa. Rāma grants Vibhīṣaṇa absolute protection.
• Sargas 22: Building of Rāma Setu: Nala and Nīla engineer the 100-yojana floating stone bridge.
• Sargas 40–108: The Great War: Slaying of Kumbhakarṇa; Lakṣmaṇa slays Indrajīt at Nikumbhilā altar; Sage Agastya imparts **Āditya Hṛdayam Stotram** to Rāma.
• Sarga 108: Slaying of Rāvaṇa: Rāma uses the Brahmāstra to pierce Rāvaṇa's heart.
• Sargas 116–128: Agni Parīkṣā & Return: Sītā's fire purity test before the Devas; return in Puṣpaka Vimāna; Coronation of Śrī Rāma (*Rāmarājya*).

Kāṇḍa 7 of 7 · 111 Sargas · ~3,000 Verses

7. Uttara Kāṇḍa (Epilogue & Return to Divine Realm)

Covers the origins of Rāvaṇa and Agastya's narratives, birth of twins Lava and Kuśa in Vālmīki's Hermitage, their chanting of Rāmāyaṇa at Rāma's Aśvamedha Yajna, Sītā's final descent into Mother Earth (*Bhūdevī*), and Śrī Rāma's ascension into Sarayū River returning to Vaikuṇṭha with all citizens of Ayodhyā.
