The 4 Goals of Human Life — Puruṣārthas

📖 Primary Sources: Manusmriti (8232.pdf) · Bhagavad Gītā · Katha Upanishad  | 📚 Read in Library →

Sanātana Dharma does not ask you to renounce the world or feel guilty about wealth or pleasure. Instead, it provides a balanced, holistic framework for a complete human life called the 4 Puruṣārthas (Purusha = human, Artha = goal/purpose).

Goal 1 · Foundation

1. Dharma (धर्म) — Righteous Duty

Dharma is the moral compass that governs all other goals. It means living with honesty, integrity, empathy, and duty toward family, society, and nature. Without Dharma, wealth turns into greed and pleasure turns into addiction.

धर्मो रक्षति रक्षितः
"Dharma protects those who protect Dharma." — Manusmriti 8.15
Goal 2 · Prosperity

2. Artha (अर्थ) — Wealth & Security

Artha means creating wealth, career success, financial stability, and material resources needed to support oneself and one's family. Wealth earned through ethical means is celebrated in Sanātana Dharma as a sacred duty.

धर्मेण सह योऽर्थः स्यात् स श्रेष्ठः
"Wealth acquired in harmony with Dharma is the highest wealth."
Goal 3 · Enjoyment

3. Kāma (काम) — Love & Joy

Kāma refers to love, emotional satisfaction, family joy, art, music, beauty, and sensory delight. Bhagavad Gītā 7.11 Krishna states: "I am that desire (Kāma) which is not contrary to Dharma."

धर्मविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ
"In all living beings, I am the desire that is in accordance with Dharma." — BG 7.11
Goal 4 · Ultimate Freedom

4. Mokṣa (मोक्ष) — Spiritual Freedom

Mokṣa is the ultimate liberation from the cycle of suffering and ignorance. It is realizing your true eternal nature as pure consciousness (Ātman) in oneness with Brahman.

तमसः मा ज्योतिर्गमय
"Lead me from darkness to light." — Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.3.28
🌿 Real-Life Analogy — Building a Sturdy House:

Think of a house: Dharma is the solid concrete foundation. Artha is the strong brick walls and roof. Kāma is the beautiful interior decoration, music, and warmth inside. Mokṣa is opening the windows to let the pure infinite sky and fresh breeze fill every room. Without a foundation, the walls crumble; without walls, interior joy cannot last!
