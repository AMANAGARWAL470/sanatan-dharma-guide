Sāṃkhya & Yoga — Consciousness & Mind Control

📖 Primary Sources: Sāṃkhya Philosophy by Nandalal Sinha (206 pp) · Rāja Yoga by Swami Vivekananda (230 pp)  | 📚 Read in Library →

Sāṃkhya (founded by Sage Kapila) is the oldest philosophical system in India. It divides all of existence into two eternal principles: Puruṣa (pure witnessing consciousness) and Prakṛti (primordial matter/nature). Yoga (founded by Patañjali) takes Sāṃkhya's theory and turns it into a practical 8-limb discipline.

Puruṣa & Prakṛti — The 25 Tattvas (Categories)

Principle 1

Puruṣa (पुरुष)

Pure, unchanging, eternal Consciousness. Unattached, peaceful, witnessing everything without getting affected.

Principle 2

Prakṛti (प्रकृति)

Creative Nature. Made of 3 Guṇas: Sattva (purity/light), Rajas (passion/movement), Tamas (inertia/darkness).

मूलप्रकृतिरविकृतिर्महदाद्याः प्रकृतिविकृतयः सप्त ।
षोडशकस्तु विकारो न प्रकृतिर्न विकृतिः पुरुषः ॥
"Root Nature (Mulaprakriti) is uncreated. The 7 principles (Mahat, Ahamkara, 5 Tanmatras) are both created and creators. The 16 products (5 senses, 5 organs of action, mind, 5 elements) are only created. Purusha is neither creator nor created."
— Sāṃkhya Kārikā, Verse 3  |  Source: thesamkhyaphilos00sinhuoft.pdf, pg. 12
