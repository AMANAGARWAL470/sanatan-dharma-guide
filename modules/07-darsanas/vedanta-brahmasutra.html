Vedānta & The Brahma Sūtras — Ultimate Reality Systematized

📖 Primary Source: Brahma Sūtra with Śaṅkara's Commentary by Swami Gambhirananda (941 pp)  | 📚 Read in Library →

Vedānta (lit. "the end/culmination of the Vedas") is the crowning glory of Indian philosophy. Maharṣi Bādarāyaṇa composed the Brahma Sūtras — 555 logical aphorisms that tie together all teachings of the Upanishads into a single, bulletproof logical system.

Sūtra 1.1.1 — The Quest Begins

The Brahma Sūtra opens with the most famous introductory aphorism in Indian philosophy:

अथातो ब्रह्मजिज्ञासा ॥
"Now, therefore, arises the inquiry into Brahman (the Ultimate Reality)."
— Brahma Sūtras 1.1.1  |  Source: Brahma_Sutra_Swami_Gambhirananda.pdf (941 pp), pg. 9

Sūtra 1.1.2 — Definition of Brahman

जन्माद्यस्य यतः ॥
"Brahman is That from which the origin, sustenance, and dissolution of this universe proceed."
— Brahma Sūtras 1.1.2  |  Source: Brahma_Sutra_Swami_Gambhirananda.pdf, pg. 18
