Nyāya & Vaiśeṣika — Logic & Ancient Atomic Theory

📖 Primary Source: Vaiśeṣika Sūtras of Kaṇāda by Nandalal Sinha (380 pp)  | 📚 Read in Library →

Long before modern science, ancient Indian philosophers created rigorous systems of physical science, chemistry, logic, and epistemology. **Nyāya** (founded by Sage Gautama) provided the 16 rules of logical debate and proof. **Vaiśeṣika** (founded by Sage Kaṇāda) proposed the world's first atomic theory (*Paramāṇuvāda*).

Kaṇāda's Atomic Theory (Paramāṇu)

Sage Kaṇāda (6th century BCE) stated that all matter can be divided into smaller particles until an indivisible, eternal particle is reached: the Paramāṇu (Atom):

सदकारणवन्नित्यम् ।
तस्य कार्यं लिङ्गम् ॥
"That which is existent, uncaused, and eternal is the Paramāṇu (Atom). Its existence is inferred from its effects (manifest matter)."
— Vaiśeṣika Sūtras 4.1.1–2  |  Source: thevaiasesikasut00kanauoft.pdf (380 pp), pg. 142
