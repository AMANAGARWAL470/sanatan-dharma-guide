The 4 Yugas & Cyclic Cosmic Time

📖 Primary Sources: Bhāgavata Purāṇa (Skandha 3 & 12) · Śiva Purāṇa · Viṣṇu Purāṇa  | 📚 Read in Library →

In modern Western thought, time is linear — it starts at a point and moves to an end. In Sanātana Dharma, time is **cyclical** — like the four seasons of a year (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter). The universe goes through continuous cycles of creation, expansion, decline, dissolution (*Pralaya*), and re-creation.

The 4 Yugas (The Great Cosmic Cycle)

Yuga 1 of 4 (48% of cycle duration)

Satya Yuga (Golden Age)

Dharma stands on 4 legs (Truth, Purity, Compassion, Austerity). Humanity lives in natural harmony, truthfulness, and direct communion with the Divine.

Yuga 2 of 4 (36% of cycle duration)

Tretā Yuga (Silver Age)

Dharma stands on 3 legs. Introduction of Yajñas and rituals. The era of Śrī Rāma and the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa.

Yuga 3 of 4 (24% of cycle duration)

Dvāpara Yuga (Bronze Age)

Dharma stands on 2 legs. Rise of passion and conflict. The era of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, Kurukṣetra War, and Mahābhārata.

Yuga 4 of 4 (12% of cycle duration)

Kali Yuga (Iron/Current Age)

Dharma stands on 1 leg (Truth). Materialism rises. But Bhāgavata Purāṇa promises: in Kali Yuga, spiritual growth is easiest through chanting God's name (*Nāma Saṅkīrtana*).

Kali Yuga Privilege — Bhāgavata Purāṇa 12.3.51

कलेर्दोषनिधे राजन्नस्ति ह्येको महान् गुणः ।
कीर्तनादेव कृष्णस्य मुक्तसङ्गः परं व्रजेत् ॥
"O King, although Kali Yuga is an ocean of faults, it has one supreme virtue: simply by chanting the name of Kṛṣṇa/God, one becomes freed from all attachment and reaches the highest goal."
— Bhāgavata Purāṇa, Skandha 12, Chapter 3, Verse 51  |  Source: Bhagavata Purana (Gita Press), pg. 1542
