1. What is Sanātana Dharma in 2 Minutes?

Unlike organized religions created by a single founder at a specific time in history, Sanātana Dharma means "Eternal Natural Law." It is the timeless truth governing the universe, human nature, and consciousness — like gravity or mathematics.

एकं सद्विप्रा बहुधा वदन्ति
"Truth is One; the wise call it by many names."
— Rig Veda 1.164.46

2. The 5 Core Concepts Every Person Must Know

1. Brahman (The Infinite Reality)

God is not an old man in the sky. Brahman is the unmanifest, infinite consciousness underlying every particle in the universe.

2. Ātman (The Immortal Self)

You are not your body or mind. The real "you" is the immortal soul (*Ātman*), which is divine in essence.

3. Karma (Cause & Effect)

Every action, thought, and word generates a reaction. You reap what you sow across lives.

4. Mokṣa (Ultimate Freedom)

The ultimate goal of life is not going to a temporary heaven, but realizing your oneness with God and being free from all suffering.

3. Top 5 Shocking Myths Busted with Proof

  • Myth: "Hindus worship 330 million Gods."
    Fact: Rig Veda says God is ONE (*Ekam Sat*). The 33 Koti Devas refers to 33 TYPES of cosmic energies (Vasus, Rudras, Adityas), NOT 33 crore gods.
  • Myth: "Caste system is birth-based as per scriptures."
    Fact: Bhagavad Gītā 4.13 states Varna is determined by quality (*Guna*) and action (*Karma*), NOT birth.
  • Myth: "Hindus worship stone idols."
    Fact: A Murti is a visible focal anchor for the omnipresent God — like looking at a picture of a loved one.

4. The 10 Avatars & Cosmic Evolution

Thousands of years before Charles Darwin, the Daśāvatāra (10 Avatars of Vishnu) described biological and spiritual evolution:

• Matsya (Fish) → Aquatic Life
• Kūrma (Tortoise) → Amphibian (Water to Land)
• Varāha (Boar) → Terrestrial Land Mammal
• Narasiṃha (Man-Lion) → Transition to Human Form
• Vāmana (Dwarf) → Early Human Intellect
• Rāma & Kṛṣṇa → Civilized Society, Moral Sovereign & Master Philosopher

5. Bhagavad Gītā in 3 Golden Verses

कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन
"You have a right to your duty, but never to the fruits of your actions. Do not let the fruits of action be your motive."
— Bhagavad Gītā 2.47
न जायते म्रियते वा कदाचिन्नायं भूत्वा भविता वा न भूयः
"The soul is never born, nor does it ever die. It is unborn, eternal, ever-existing, and primeval."
— Bhagavad Gītā 2.20

7. Simple 10-Minute Daily Practice

  1. Morning (3 Mins): Sit quietly, chant "ॐ" (AUM) 3 times, feel the vibration.
  2. Daytime (4 Mins): Perform your work with total focus without worrying about results (*Karma Yoga*).
  3. Evening (3 Mins): Recite the Gāyatrī Mantra or Mahāmṛtyuñjaya Mantra for peace.
