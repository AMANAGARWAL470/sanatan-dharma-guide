# Key Patterns & Principles — 108 Upanishads

## 1. Cosmic Order (Ṛta & Dharma)
- The universe is self-sustaining through natural harmony and moral order.
- Action aligned with Dharma produces inner clarity and spiritual freedom.

## 2. The Identity of Self (Ātman & Brahman)
- The individual consciousness (Ātman) is non-different from the Universal Consciousness (Brahman).

## 3. The Law of Karma
- Every intentional thought and action generates a natural resonance and result.
