# Quick Reference Cheatsheet — Brahma Sutras

| Concept | Definition / Verse | Key Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Satya** | Truthfulness | Absolute Reality & Moral Integrity |
| **Dharma** | Cosmic Righteousness | Upholding law and duty |
| **Ahimsa** | Non-violence | Harm to none in thought, word, or deed |
| **Moksha** | Liberation | Freedom from cycle of rebirth |

## Core Rule
> *"Walk on the path of Ṛta and Truth, and you shall prosper."*
