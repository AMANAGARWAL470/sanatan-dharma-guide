# Glossary — Vaisheshika Sutras

- **Ātman**: The eternal individual soul / self.
- **Brahman**: The ultimate, infinite, unchanging reality.
- **Dharma**: Natural law, duty, righteousness, and order.
- **Karma**: Action and its natural law of consequence.
- **Moksha**: Spiritual liberation and ultimate freedom.
- **Ṛta**: The eternal cosmic order governing the universe.
- **Yajna**: Sacred selfless sacrifice or dedicated action.
